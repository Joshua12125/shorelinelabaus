Independent testing

Methods you may see on a COA

Laboratories use different analytical methods. These are the ones most commonly reported for research peptides. The tests shown on any given report depend on the laboratory and the batch, so always read the individual COA.

Purity

HPLC

High-performance liquid chromatography separates a sample into components and reports the main compound as a percentage.

Identity

Mass spectrometry

Measures molecular mass to confirm the material matches the named compound.

Quantity

Content by assay

Reports the measured amount per vial so it can be compared with the labelled amount.

Safety screen

Endotoxin (LAL)

Detects bacterial endotoxin. Some laboratories include it, others do not.

Impurities

Residual solvents & metals

Screens for leftover solvents and trace metals (ICP-MS) where those tests are ordered.

Moisture

Water content

Karl Fischer titration measures moisture in lyophilised powder.

COA guide

How to read a COA

Layouts differ between laboratories, but most reports contain the same core fields.

FieldWhat it tells you
Laboratory nameWhich independent lab ran the analysis. It should be named clearly and be possible to look up.
Test / report IDA unique reference used to pull up the original report on the lab's verification page.
Batch or sample referenceLinks the report to a specific batch so results are not reused across batches.
Test dateWhen the sample was analysed. Newer batches should have newer reports.
Purity (HPLC)Share of the sample that is the main compound, shown as a percentage.
Identity (mass spectrometry)Confirms the measured molecular mass matches the expected compound.
QuantityMeasured amount per vial against the labelled amount.
Appearance / formatPhysical description, for example lyophilised powder.

Research standards

Handling, records and naming

Storage and handling basics

Research materials are generally supplied as lyophilised powder. Requirements vary by compound, so follow the guidance on the product page and label.

  • Store as directed, commonly refrigerated at 2–8°C
  • Keep sealed, labelled and away from light and heat
  • Use clean laboratory technique and protective equipment
  • Store securely, away from unauthorised persons

Records and nomenclature

Good records make research repeatable, and clear names avoid mix-ups between similar compounds.

  • Record the batch number and report ID for every experiment
  • Keep a copy of the COA with your lab records
  • Confirm the compound name on the report matches your order
  • Ask us if a name, batch or quantity looks inconsistent

Quick answers

Frequently asked questions

What does "research use only" mean?
It means the material is supplied for use in a laboratory by qualified persons. It is not intended to be consumed, injected, applied or administered to humans or animals, and it is not a medicine or supplement.
Where can I find the COA for my batch?
Published reports are listed on our COAs page, with links to the testing laboratory's verification page.
What if the COA for my batch is not published yet?
Testing is rolled out batch by batch and reports are published as they become available. Contact us with your batch reference and we will tell you where it stands.
What is HPLC purity?
HPLC (high-performance liquid chromatography) separates a sample into its components. The report shows the share of the sample that is the main compound, as a percentage.
What does mass spectrometry confirm?
It measures the molecular mass of the material. If that matches the expected mass for the named compound, it supports the identity of the sample.
Is a COA a guarantee of safety or suitability for any use?
No. A COA reports analytical results for a specific batch. It does not make a material safe or suitable for human or animal use, and it is not an approval by any regulator.
Do you provide medical or usage advice?
No. We do not provide medical advice, dosing guidance or instructions for use on people or animals. Our materials are for laboratory research only.
Do I need to be 18 or older?
Yes. You must be 18 years or older to view our site and purchase from us.
